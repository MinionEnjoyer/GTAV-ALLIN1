# Complete vehicle listings

The built-in GBAY roster covers all 935 distinct Rockstar vehicle models in the
September 6, 2026 local archive audit: 463 existing static entries, 256 Story
entries (two overlapping), and 218 additional catalog-only entries.

Catalog-only entries appear in All and their category, support name/model search,
and show **CATALOG ONLY / Not offered yet** in the Reactor UI. They have no enabled
purchase or delivery action, including in free mode. Zero is an internal unused
price placeholder, not an offer of a free vehicle. Existing traffic pools,
purchase prices, preview dictionary ordering and hangar restrictions are unchanged.
These entries do not grant mission/getaway authority or package traffic access.

The existing Story catalog includes the Epsilon reward tractor (`tractor`), Space
Docker (`dune2`), and Devin Weston's collection models (`cheetah`, `entityxf`,
`ztype`, `jb700`, `monroe`). Search recognizes Devin/Devon Weston, Epsilon, cult,
Michael, UFO, alien, Omega and Space Docker where appropriate. Native localized
vehicle names remain preferred; corrected fallback names are retained in the data.

Mission colors, plates, extras, weapon states and reward behavior are not separate
model IDs. This change lists the models; it does not recreate those scripted
presets, award mission completion, or create duplicate variants of the same model.

`data/catalog_only_vehicles.json` is the browse-only supplement. Its checked-in
runtime counterpart is `script/src/CatalogOnlyVehicles.cs`, generated by
`allin1.generators.catalog_only_vehicles`. Tests require the generated file to
match its input and the complete union to equal `catalog/vehicle_seats.json`.
When a model becomes purchasable, remove it from this supplement and add its
reviewed price and supported storage route to the regular catalog. Do not change
the complete-roster assertion merely to hide omissions.

No new garages, hangars, traffic participation, screenshots, mission presets or
in-game acceptance claims are introduced. The previous audit remains a dated
before-change snapshot. This change is not an assertion that every model streams
or operates correctly in both editions.
